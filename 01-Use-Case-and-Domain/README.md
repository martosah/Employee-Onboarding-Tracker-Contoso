# 1. Use Case and Domain

## What the System Is

The Employee Onboarding Tracker is a Power Platform solution that manages every task required to take a new hire from offer acceptance to a fully productive employee at the end of their probation period.

It replaces the spreadsheet-based tracking currently used by Contoso HR, gives every onboarding task a clear owner and due date, and provides visibility to HR leadership on the status of every case in flight.

---

## The Domain

Employee onboarding spans four operational areas inside the organisation:

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| HR | Owns the case end-to-end, schedules induction, captures personal documents |
| IT | Provisions laptop, email and Active Directory account, software access |
| Facilities | Allocates desk and access card, ships equipment to remote hires |
| Compliance | Prepares contracts, NDAs, mandatory training enrolment |

The system coordinates the handoffs between these four areas so that nothing is dropped between them.

---

## What It Covers

- Capturing new hire details when an offer is accepted
- Generating the standard onboarding checklist tailored to the new hire's role
- Assigning tasks to the right people in HR, IT, Facilities, and Compliance
- Tracking task status and equipment issued
- Allowing new hires to upload required personal documents before Day 1
- Confirming Day-1 readiness and flagging gaps in advance
- Tracking 30/60/90-day probation milestones
- Closing the case once probation is passed

---

## What It Does Not Cover

- Recruitment and applicant tracking (handled by a separate ATS)
- Payroll setup and benefits enrolment (handled by the HRIS)
- Performance management beyond the probation period
- Offboarding (a separate use case)
