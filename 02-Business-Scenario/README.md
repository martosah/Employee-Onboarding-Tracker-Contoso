
# Business Scenario

## Contoso Today

Contoso onboards more than 30 new employees every month across multiple departments. The HR team currently coordinates onboarding through a shared Excel tracker on SharePoint, with informal email handoffs to IT, Facilities, and Compliance.

## Pain Points

| Problem | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| No formal task ownership | Tasks slip through the cracks because no single person is accountable |
| No automatic notifications | IT, Facilities, and Compliance are unaware of upcoming start dates until HR chases them |
| No escalation when tasks are overdue | Managers and HR leadership only find out about gaps when the new hire arrives |
| No central record of equipment issued | Laptops and access cards are tracked separately, with frequent disputes |
| No audit trail | Compliance cannot evidence who did what and when |
| Limited visibility for leadership | Head of HR cannot see how many cases are in flight, at risk, or overdue |
| Poor new hire experience | New starters arrive on Day 1 without laptops, accounts, or access |

## What "Better" Looks Like

A single system where every onboarding case is created automatically when an offer is signed, every task has an owner and a due date, every stakeholder is notified, every gap is flagged before Day 1, and HR leadership has full visibility throughout.
