# 2. Business Scenario

## Contoso Today

Contoso onboards more than 30 new employees every month across multiple departments — Customer Experience, Technology, Operations, Marketing, Finance, and others. The HR team currently coordinates onboarding through a shared Excel tracker on SharePoint, with informal email handoffs to IT, Facilities, and Compliance.

The process works on paper. In practice, it depends on individual diligence — the HR Business Partner (HRBP) remembering to email IT, IT remembering to prepare the laptop, Facilities remembering to assign a desk. When anything is missed, the new hire feels it on Day 1.

---

## Pain Points

| Problem | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| No formal task ownership | Tasks slip through the cracks because no single person is accountable |
| No automatic notifications | IT, Facilities, and Compliance are unaware of upcoming start dates until HR chases them |
| No escalation when tasks are overdue | Managers and HR leadership only find out about gaps when the new hire arrives |
| No central record of equipment issued | Laptops and access cards are tracked separately, with frequent disputes over who got what |
| No audit trail | Compliance cannot evidence who did what and when |
| Limited visibility for leadership | Head of HR cannot see how many cases are in flight, at risk, or overdue |
| Spreadsheet version conflicts | Multiple HRBPs editing the same file overwrite each other's updates |
| Poor new hire experience | New starters arrive on Day 1 without laptops, accounts, or access |

---

## Business Impact

The pain points translate into measurable business cost:

- **Time-to-productivity is delayed.** New hires lose their first few days waiting for access, equipment, and accounts.
- **HR effort is consumed by chasing.** HRBPs spend hours each week following up on tasks that should have been completed automatically.
- **Compliance risk increases.** Without an audit trail, the organisation cannot prove that mandatory training, NDAs, and background checks were completed before access was granted.
- **First impressions are damaged.** A poor onboarding experience signals to new hires that the organisation is disorganised, affecting retention from week one.

---

## What "Better" Looks Like

A single system where every onboarding record is created automatically when an offer is signed, every task has an owner and a due date, every stakeholder is notified, every gap is flagged before Day 1, and HR leadership has full visibility throughout. Personal documents are uploaded by the new hire before they arrive. The audit trail is automatic. Closing the onboarding record requires nothing more than the manager confirming the probation outcome at the 90-day mark.
