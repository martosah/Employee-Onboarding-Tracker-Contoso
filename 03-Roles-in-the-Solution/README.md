# 3. Roles in the Solution

The system serves seven roles. Each role has a defined scope of access and responsibility.

---

## 1. HR Business Partner (HRBP)

Owns onboarding record end-to-end. Submits the New Hire Form when an offer is signed, monitors progress, chases gaps, and approves uploaded documents. Each new hire is assigned to one HRBP for the duration of their onboarding.

---

## 2. Head of HR

Has full visibility across all onboardings. Reviews dashboards, monitors performance metrics, and steps in when escalations require senior HR intervention. Can reopen closed onboarding records when corrections are needed.

---

## 3. IT Administrator

Executes IT-related onboarding tasks: laptop preparation, email and Active Directory account creation, software and system access provisioning. Records equipment serial numbers when issuing assets.

---

## 4. Facilities Officer

Executes facilities tasks: desk allocation, physical and digital access card issuance, office orientation. For remote hires, arranges courier shipping of equipment to the new hire's home address.

---

## 5. Compliance Officer

Executes compliance tasks: contract preparation, NDA execution, background check confirmation, regulatory training enrolment. Maintains the compliance record for each new hire.

---

## 6. Reporting Manager

The new hire's direct line manager. Reviews their team's onboarding progress.

---

## 7. New Hire (External User)

Accesses a self-service portal to view their own onboarding progress, see Day-1 logistics (start time, location, manager, induction details), and upload required personal documents — BVN, NIN, academic certificates, bank account details — before their start date.

---

## Role-to-Task Mapping

| Task Type | Default Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome pack, induction scheduling, document approval | HR Business Partner |
| Laptop, email, AD account, software access | IT Administrator |
| Desk, access card, office tour, equipment shipping | Facilities Officer |
| Contract, NDA, background check, regulatory training | Compliance Officer |
| Onboarding check progress in, training of new hires | Reporting Manager |
| Document upload, profile review | New Hire |
