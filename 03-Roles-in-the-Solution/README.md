# Roles in the Solution

The system serves six roles. Each role has a defined scope of access and responsibility.

## 1. HR Business Partner (HRBP)

Owns onboarding cases end-to-end. Submits the New Hire Form when an offer is signed, monitors progress, and chases gaps. Each new hire is assigned to one HRBP.

## 2. Head of HR

Has full visibility across all onboarding cases. Reviews dashboards, monitors performance metrics, and steps in when escalations require senior HR intervention.

## 3. IT Administrator

Executes IT-related onboarding tasks: laptop preparation, email and Active Directory account creation, software access provisioning. Tracks equipment issued.

## 4. Facilities Officer

Executes facilities tasks: desk allocation, access card issuance, office orientation. For remote hires, arranges shipping of equipment.

## 5. Compliance Officer

Executes compliance tasks: contract preparation, NDA execution, background check confirmation, regulatory training enrolment.

## 6. Reporting Manager

Reviews their direct reports' onboarding progress. Owns the 30/60/90-day probation milestones and records the final probation outcome.

## 7. New Hire (External User)

Accesses a self-service portal to view their own onboarding progress, see Day-1 logistics, and upload required personal documents (BVN, NIN, academic certificates, bank details) before their start date.
