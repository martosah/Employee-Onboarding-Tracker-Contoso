# 4. Key Stakeholders

Stakeholders are people or groups affected by the solution but who do not necessarily use it daily. They influence decisions about the system or are impacted by its outcomes.

---

## Internal Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Chief HR Officer | Quality and consistency of onboarding across the organisation |
| Chief Information Officer | IT readiness for new hires; security and access governance |
| Chief Operating Officer | Time-to-productivity for new starters; operational efficiency |
| Department Heads | Their own teams' new hires being ready on Day 1 |
| Existing Employees | Colleagues who depend on new hires being onboarded smoothly to share the workload |

---

## Governance Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Compliance | Audit trail of contracts, NDAs, and mandatory training completion |
| Internal Audit | Evidence of consistent process execution and segregation of duties |
| Data Protection Officer | Lawful handling of new hire personal data (BVN, NIN, bank details) |
| Information Security | Access provisioning controls, joiner-mover-leaver process integrity |

---

## External Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| New Hires | Smooth, professional onboarding experience |
| Equipment Vendors | Visibility into equipment requests for fulfilment planning |
| Background Check Providers | Coordination on candidate verification before Day 1 |
| Courier Services | Equipment delivery scheduling for remote hires |

---

## Stakeholder Influence

- **High influence, high interest** — Chief HR Officer, Chief Information Officer, Compliance, Data Protection Officer. Engaged closely throughout design and rollout.
- **High influence, lower interest** — Chief Operating Officer, Information Security. Kept informed at key milestones.
- **Lower influence, high interest** — Department Heads, New Hires, Reporting Managers. Consulted on user experience and workflow design.
- **Lower influence, lower interest** — External vendors, courier services. Engaged transactionally as the system needs them.
