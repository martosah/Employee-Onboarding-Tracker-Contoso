
# Key Stakeholders

Stakeholders are people or groups affected by the solution but who do not necessarily use it daily.

## Internal Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Chief HR Officer | Quality and consistency of onboarding across the organisation |
| Chief Information Officer | IT readiness for new hires; security and access governance |
| Chief Operating Officer | Time-to-productivity for new starters; operational efficiency |
| Department Heads | Their own teams' new hires being ready on Day 1 |
| Existing Employees | Indirect — colleagues who depend on new hires being onboarded smoothly |

## Governance Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Compliance | Audit trail of contracts, NDAs, and mandatory training completion |
| Internal Audit | Evidence of consistent process execution and segregation of duties |
| Data Protection Officer | Lawful handling of new hire personal data (BVN, NIN, bank details) |
| Information Security | Access provisioning and de-provisioning controls |

## External Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| New Hires | Smooth, professional onboarding experience |
| Equipment Vendors | Visibility into equipment requests for fulfilment planning |
| Background Check Providers | Coordination on candidate verification before Day 1 |
